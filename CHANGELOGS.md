### 1.5.0 (2026-10-23)

- Requires MerlinLib 0.9.1 or newer.
- Shipped for 1.20.1 (Forge), 1.21.1 (NeoForge), 26.1 - 26.2 (NeoForge, Fabric) and 26.3 (NeoForge, Fabric).
- Released under CC0-1.0.
- Incinerate: a mob killed by it leaves no loot behind and no longer splits.
- Twilight Slayer: the night bonus applies in every dimension again, not only in the Twilight Forest.
- Godwood Aura: doubled mob drops and a chance of an extra ambrosium shard when breaking blocks; it cannot sit next to Incinerate or Looting.
- Four enchantments for the Twilight Forest and the Aether, registered only when their mod is installed.
- The vanilla enchantment adjustments can be switched on and off from the server configuration.

### 1.4.0 (2026-10-21)

- Requires MerlinLib 0.9.0 or newer.
- Shipped for 1.20.1 (Forge), 1.21.1 (NeoForge), 26.1 - 26.2 (NeoForge, Fabric) and 26.3 (NeoForge, Fabric).
- Bright: at night a helmet now loses one durability every 13.75 seconds instead of every second, so it lasts a night.
- Chinese: taking the chestplate off in creative mode no longer takes the flight away.

### 1.3.2 (2026-10-04)

- Requires MerlinLib 0.8.5 or newer.
- The join notice is now sent through MerlinLib's notice feature, so it follows that mod's master switch and per mod switches instead of a switch of its own.
- Exclusive set tags are written as separate members, which restores the mutual exclusion between the affected enchantments.
