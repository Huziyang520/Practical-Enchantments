### 1.7.0 (2026-11-04)

- Requires MerlinLib 1.1.0 or newer.
- Shipped for 1.20.1 (Forge), 1.21.1 (NeoForge), 26.1 - 26.2 (NeoForge, Fabric) and 26.3 (NeoForge, Fabric).
- New enchantments: Swift Arrow, Trauma, Hide Skin, Repeating Crossbow and Hunting Arrow.
- New effects: Bleed (stacks up to 5, bursts on the fifth) and Rot (stacking damage that wears armour down).
- Powerful now knocks targets back and makes them bleed instead of adding damage. Its in-game description
  changed accordingly.
- Hide Skin keeps you off the powder-snow sinking and stops freezing damage while worn.
- Hunting Arrow turns your arrows into Twilight Forest seeker arrows.
- New toggles: Quick Charge on bows, Power on crossbows, Thorns on shields.
- Infinity now prefers spectral arrows over ordinary ones when both are in the inventory.
- Bright now consumes one durability every 27.5 seconds of night instead of 13.75.
- Ember Quench and Twilight Slayer now really come out of Twilight Forest chests.

### 1.6.1 (2026-10-28)

- Requires MerlinLib 1.0.0 or newer.
- Shipped for 1.20.1 (Forge), 1.21.1 (NeoForge), 26.1 - 26.2 (NeoForge, Fabric) and 26.3 (NeoForge, Fabric).
- Provocation and Heavy Blow are sold by librarians and can be found in chests again.
- Invisible Air is no longer unobtainable: mobs spawn holding a sword that carries it, and it drops with the sword.
- Beheading, Frenzy, Life Steal, Soul Siphon, Venom, Penetration, Smashing, Wither Aspect, Demonic Pact and
  Execution now really show up in the chests the lists always promised.
- Bright, Lumberjack, Powerful and Aerial Haste no longer come from the enchanting table, as designed.
- Swords, axes and spears can all carry the melee enchantments.

### 1.6.0 (2026-10-27)

- Requires MerlinLib 1.0.0 or newer.
- Shipped for 1.20.1 (Forge), 1.21.1 (NeoForge), 26.1 - 26.2 (NeoForge, Fabric) and 26.3 (NeoForge, Fabric).
- New enchantments: Provocation, Heavy Blow, Invisible Air and Obsidian Walker.
- New items: iron, golden, diamond and netherite amulets. Armour enchantments work while an amulet is worn
  in a trinket slot or held in the offhand, and upgrading an amulet keeps every enchantment on it.
- Flawed obsidian: left behind by Obsidian Walker, it cracks in three stages and melts back into lava, and
  mining it turns it into lava as well. It covers as wide an area as Frost Walker does.
- Wrath: a new potion, its splash, lingering and tipped-arrow forms, and the brewing chain that leads to it.
- The amulet price in an anvil stays low, so reinforcing one never runs into "Too Expensive".
- Settings can also be edited from MezzConfig when it is installed; it is not required.

### 1.5.0 (2026-10-23)

- Requires MerlinLib 0.9.1 or newer.
- Shipped for 1.20.1 (Forge), 1.21.1 (NeoForge), 26.1 - 26.2 (NeoForge, Fabric) and 26.3 (NeoForge, Fabric).
- Released under CC0-1.0.
- Incinerate: a mob killed by it leaves no loot behind and no longer splits.
- Twilight Slayer: the night bonus applies in every dimension again, not only in the Twilight Forest.
- Godwood Aura: doubled mob drops and a chance of an extra ambrosium shard when breaking blocks; it cannot sit next to Incinerate or Looting.
- Four enchantments for the Twilight Forest and the Aether, registered only when their mod is installed.
- The vanilla enchantment adjustments can be switched on and off from the server configuration.

### 1.4.0 (2026-10-21)

- Requires MerlinLib 0.9.0 or newer.
- Shipped for 1.20.1 (Forge), 1.21.1 (NeoForge), 26.1 - 26.2 (NeoForge, Fabric) and 26.3 (NeoForge, Fabric).

### 1.3.2 (2026-10-04)

- Requires MerlinLib 0.8.5 or newer.
- The join notice is now sent through MerlinLib's notice feature, so it follows that mod's master switch and per mod switches instead of a switch of its own.
- Exclusive set tags are written as separate members, which restores the mutual exclusion between the affected enchantments.
